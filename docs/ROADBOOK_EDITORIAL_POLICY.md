# Purpose

This document defines how locations, stops and recommendations are selected for the Norway Road Trip Companion.

The objective is not to include the maximum number of attractions.

The objective is to create the most enjoyable and stress-free family road trip.

Every recommendation must improve the journey.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

Less is more.

Every stop must earn its place.

If a recommendation does not improve the journey, it should not appear in the Roadbook.

---

# Primary Objective

The family should arrive relaxed.

The Roadbook is designed to reduce unnecessary driving, unnecessary decisions and unnecessary stops.

---

# Stop Selection Rules

Every stop must satisfy the following criteria.

## Rule 1

Stay on the planned route.

Avoid unnecessary detours.

Target:

0–3 minutes additional driving.

Maximum:

5 minutes.

Exceptions are allowed only for truly exceptional experiences.

---

## Rule 2

Never backtrack.

The Roadbook should never recommend:

• U-turns

• repeated roads

• driving back to previous locations

unless unavoidable.

---

## Rule 3

One stop should provide multiple benefits.

Preferred examples

✓ Charging + Breakfast

✓ Picnic + Viewpoint

✓ Coffee + Local Bakery

✓ Parking + Waterfall

Avoid single-purpose stops whenever possible.

---

## Rule 4

Easy parking is mandatory.

Every stop should have

• official parking

• easy access

• enough space for two vehicles

• simple exit back to the planned route

---

## Rule 5

Family first.

Prefer locations with

• toilets

• picnic tables

• children friendly

• easy walking

• safe environment

---

## Rule 6

Time efficiency.

Every stop should naturally fit the day's rhythm.

Example

Breakfast while charging

instead of

Charging followed by separate breakfast stop.

---

## Rule 7

Homemade food is the default.

The Roadbook assumes the family carries homemade food.

Restaurant recommendations should complement packed meals rather than replace them.

Examples

✔ Coffee only

✔ Dessert only

✔ Local speciality

✔ Ice cream

✔ Save packed lunch for later

---

## Rule 8

Local Discovery is curated.

Only include businesses that genuinely add value.

Never recommend something simply because it has high ratings.

Each recommendation must explain

• Why it is famous

• Why it fits today's itinerary

• Vegetarian suitability

• Estimated additional time

---

## Rule 9

No unnecessary attractions.

Do not stop because

• it is famous

• it is Instagram popular

• it has many reviews

Stop because it improves today's journey.

---

## Rule 10

Weather resilience.

Every important outdoor stop should have a nearby indoor alternative.

The day's itinerary should never need major replanning because of rain.

---

# Roadbook Rating

The Roadbook Rating is not a Google rating.

It reflects how suitable the stop is for THIS itinerary.

★★★★★ Don't Miss

★★★★☆ Excellent

★★★☆☆ Good if Time Allows

★★☆☆☆ Optional

★☆☆☆☆ Skip Today

Rating factors

• Route alignment

• Parking

• Family suitability

• Time efficiency

• Scenic value

• Local authenticity

• Vegetarian friendliness

• Weather suitability

---

# Journey Rhythm

Every day should follow a natural flow.

Morning

↓

Drive

↓

Coffee & Charging

↓

Scenic Experience

↓

Picnic / Lunch

↓

Drive

↓

Destination

Avoid repeating the same type of stop.

---

# Decision Principle

Whenever there is doubt between two locations:

Choose the option that

• requires less driving

• requires less walking

• has easier parking

• fits the itinerary better

• creates a better family experience

rather than the more famous attraction.

---

# Final Principle

The Norway Road Trip Companion is not trying to show everything Norway has to offer.

It is helping one family enjoy one unforgettable road trip.

Every recommendation should feel intentional, practical and worth the time.
